Write it once.

Most goals die in the notes app. Typed on a whim, buried under a thousand other things, never looked at again.

A hardcover journal changes the contract. Pen on paper is slower, and that is the point. You decide what is worth writing down. You commit to it in ink. There is no backspace, no undo, no rough draft.

ONE LIFE. NO ROUGH DRAFTS. is the standard you set for the page, and then for the day.

Why paper

A goal you can see
is a goal you can chase.

Research on goal setting points the same way. In a study led by Dr. Gail Matthews at Dominican University of California, people who wrote their goals down, made action commitments and reported their progress did better than people who only thought about their goals.

A journal is where all three happen. The goal. The weekly action. The honest check-in.
